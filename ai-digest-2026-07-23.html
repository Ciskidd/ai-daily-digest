White House names Moonshot AI: alleges Kimi K3 was distilled from Anthropic's Fable, trained on banned GB300s via Thailand Follow-upChinaPolicy

CyberScoop · CNBC · The Register — Jul 23, 2026
OSTP Director Michael Kratsios publicly accused Moonshot AI of building its Kimi K3 model through "large-scale industrial distillation" of Anthropic's Fable, saying the startup ran an internal platform that rotated across access methods to evade detection. He further alleged Moonshot acquired GB300-equipped servers and tapped GB300 capacity in Thailand to train models despite U.S. export bans. It is the first time the U.S. government has named a specific Chinese lab over distillation — escalating Anthropic's February claims against DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax into an official enforcement posture.
Why it matters: Moves the chip-smuggling and distillation debate from private complaint to named government accusation, tightening the case for stricter enforcement and third-country diversion controls.
