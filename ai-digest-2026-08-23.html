Google's Open Gemma Models Cross 1 Billion Downloads

Google DeepMind·Aug 21

Google DeepMind says its open Gemma family has passed one billion downloads, with developers publishing 100,000+ variants and fine-tunes since launch ~two years ago. It's also launching an “Awesome Gemma” GitHub repo to organize community projects. Gemma runs on cloud, laptops, edge devices and specialized hardware.

Why it matters Open-model ecosystems are proving as strategic as frontier models in the US–China AI race.

Nvidia: Simple Linear Math Can Replace Costly AI Model Handoffs

VentureBeat·Aug 21–22
