OpenAI to cut off Cursor after SpaceX acquisition, ending model access Nov 12

CNBC·Aug 29

OpenAI said it will terminate the contract that supplies its models to AI coding platform Cursor, following Cursor's ~$60B acquisition by Elon Musk's SpaceX. Access ends November 12, 2026, the contractual notice period. OpenAI said it doubts a Musk-owned company will honor its terms of service, citing past violations by Twitter and xAI, and confirmed future models such as Astra will not be added to Cursor.

Why it matters Model providers are now willing to sever a top distribution channel over ownership and trust — a new axis of competitive risk for every app built on someone else's API.

Qualcomm angleNeutral

Minimal direct read. Second-order: reinforces that the app layer is hostage to model-provider terms, which strengthens the case for on-device/local inference independence that Qualcomm markets, but far removed from this specific dispute.

Investor angleMixed

Bearish for single-supplier app plays (Cursor loses OpenAI; must lean on Anthropic/open models); bullish for model diversification vendors and Anthropic's coding share. Signals platform-risk repricing across the AI app stack. Not investment advice.

Anthropic signs out Claude users after infostealer malware hijacks login sessions

BleepingComputer·Aug 31

Anthropic disclosed that attackers used commodity infostealers (Vidar, Lumma, StealC, RedLine, Acreed on Windows; Atomic Stealer on macOS) to lift authenticated Claude session cookies from infected PCs and burn victims' paid usage — bypassing 2FA and SSO entirely.
