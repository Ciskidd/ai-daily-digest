MiniMax open-sources H3, an omni-modal model generating 2K/15s video with native stereo audio

译 Translated from Chinese MiniMax 稀宇科技 (official) · open-sourced Aug 3, 2026 · also: MarkTechPost, Hugging Face

稀宇科技（MiniMax）开源 H3（海螺 3.0）：统一理解文本、图像、视频、音频的全模态模型，可生成最长 15 秒、2K 分辨率、带原生立体声的视频。

MiniMax open-sourced H3 (Hailuo 3.0), a general-purpose "omni-modal" model that jointly understands text, images, video and audio in one context and outputs video with native stereo audio at up to 2K and 15 seconds. It is built on a 33.1B dense single-stream omni-transformer with a Qwen3-VL-32B text encoder; the open release ships H3-Base checkpoints (text/first-last-frame-to-video and reference-based generation) under a community license permitting commercial use for organizations under $20M revenue. It extends China's cost-led, open-weights push into frontier generative video.

Why it matters: Audio-native, open-weights 2K video at this size lands the generative-video frontier in developers' hands — and keeps pricing pressure on closed video models.
Qualcomm angle
Watch — Video generation this heavy runs in the cloud, not on a handset, so no near-term Snapdragon pull. Longer term, cheaper open multimodal models seed on-device inference demand across the Chinese OEMs Qualcomm serves, offset by Beijing's domestic-silicon push.
Investor angle
Mixed — Bearish for pricing power at closed video-model vendors (Runway, Google Veo, OpenAI Sora); bullish for the open ecosystem and cloud GPU demand to serve it. Overlaid with export-control and geopolitical risk on Chinese labs. Not investment advice.

OpenAI + academic partners · Aug 1, 2026

A field report from OpenAI and academic collaborators found AI coding agents modernized and optimized aging scientific software with speedups of up to 60×.
