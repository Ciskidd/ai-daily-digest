EU Designates ChatGPT a ‘Very Large Online Search Engine’ — the First AI Chatbot Under the DSA’s Toughest Tier

BNN Bloomberg·Aug 31 Top Story

The European Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) under the Digital Services Act — the first standalone AI chatbot to enter the EU’s strictest online-safety tier, on the basis that its EU audience exceeds the 45M monthly-active-user threshold. OpenAI must now run formal systemic-risk assessments and independent audits, and give vetted researchers and regulators conditional data access, with a compliance deadline at the end of December 2026. Reddit and Roblox were designated Very Large Online Platforms the same day.

Why it matters It’s the first time a general-purpose AI chatbot is regulated like core internet infrastructure — a template that will extend to every large model with an EU consumer footprint.

Pentagon Wires ChatGPT and Grok Into GenAI.mil for 3M Personnel — Claude Still Absent After Its Court Win

TechCrunch·Aug 31 Top Story Follow-up
Follow-up to the Aug 28 court ruling (covered Aug 31) that blocked the Pentagon’s Anthropic blacklisting. New today: ChatGPT and Grok are now live on GenAI.mil — yet Claude is still not on the portal despite the ruling.

The DoD added OpenAI’s ChatGPT Mil and xAI’s Grok for Government to GenAI.mil, its secure portal that already offered Google Gemini, giving roughly 3M military and civilian personnel access to frontier models without routing sensitive data through consumer channels. Notably, Anthropic’s Claude remains off the portal — even though a federal judge on Aug 28 blocked the Pentagon’s ‘supply-chain risk’ blacklisting of Anthropic as ‘illegal and baseless.’ The dispute stems from Anthropic’s refusal to permit Claude’s use for domestic surveillance or autonomous weapons; the government is expected to appeal.

Why it matters The largest single US-government AI deployment is consolidating around OpenAI, xAI and Google — and, court ruling notwithstanding, still routing around the one lab that pushed hardest on safety terms.
