Google ships Gemini 3.7 Flash, a cheaper coding-and-agent workhorse

Reuters·Aug 14

Just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash, calling it its most intelligent 'workhorse' model for software engineering, knowledge work and agent tasks. Coding benchmarks jumped from 34.4% to 43.6% on FrontierCode 1.1 and from 49% to 65.3% on DeepSWE v1.1, with a 1M-token context window. Introductory pricing of $0.75/$3.75 per million input/output tokens through year-end halves the prior Flash cost.

Why it matters Google is iterating faster and cutting price to win the everyday agent/coding workload that actually drives inference revenue.

No direct silicon read. Second-order: cheaper, faster cloud agent models raise the bar for what on-device NPUs must match, pressuring the on-device inference thesis Snapdragon leans on for phones and PCs.
